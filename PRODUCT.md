# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static) + plain CSS with custom properties, Cloudflare Pages + Pages Functions, D1, Resend, Bunny Stream, Stripe Checkout + Tribute. Pinned in docs/SPEC.md.

## Users

Women (mostly) from Katerina's Instagram (5.2k) and Telegram audience, arriving on a phone from a bio link or story. They slouch, feel stiffness in the lower back, chest and neck, and want to "learn how to straighten up properly" without a heavy training program. RU primary; EN secondary.

## Product Purpose

Sell one inexpensive recorded product: the mini-course "3 lessons on posture" (~50 min total, follow-along). Goal: first sales and a buyer base. No upsell yet. Success = visitor understands the offer within one screen and pays in under a minute.

## Positioning

A posture coach's proven entry lesson set (formerly the free masterclass before her MOVA course), sold cheaply, delivered by a personal link with no account. Promises a skill and a felt sensation, not cosmetic transformation.

## Operating Context

Lessons: 01 mobility of hips/pelvis and chest (~10 min), 02 basic self-correction (~25 min), 03 consolidation under load (~15 min). Optional equipment: 2–3 kg dumbbells or water bottles, 2 sticks or mops. Payment: Stripe (EUR, all countries), Tribute (RUB, Russia). Access arrives on the page and by email right after payment; lifetime.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Price lives in config. Launch price recommended €9; undecided (€9 or €18). RUB price undecided.
- Download vs stream-only undecided. EN subtitles undecided.
- Legal: Katerina is self-employed (Latvia, to confirm); footer requisites pending.
- Almost all traffic is mobile, including Instagram in-app browser.

## Brand Commitments

- Name/wordmark: MOVA (site MovaKT). Katerina Khimicheva, posture coach, Riga + online.
- Katerina's rule: the less busy, the better.
- Reference for structure: fullbodyclub.ru — section labels like "/ для кого /", flat structure, simple typography.
- Copy is Katerina's own (worked on the masterclass); edit only headline, appearance promises and typos.
- Headline chosen: «Первые шаги к ровной осанке: 3 урока, которые можно повторять дома».

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. Photos, review screenshots, About text and videos come later from Katerina. Until then: neutral placeholders. Never invent testimonials, student counts or years of experience.

## Product Principles

1. Promise only what 50 minutes can deliver: sensation and skill.
2. Zero friction to buy and to watch: no account, no password.
3. Quiet beats loud: one accent, no clutter.
4. Mobile first, Instagram in-app browser first.
